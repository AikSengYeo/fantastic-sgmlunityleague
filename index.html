Our Purpose

More Than Football

The proposed SGML Unity Football League 2027 aims to build an inclusive community where players, teams and supporters from different backgrounds can connect through the beautiful game. The focus is on friendship, respect, healthy ageing and lasting community relationships.

Consultation notice: This website presents a proposal for discussion. Competition formats, eligibility, dates, fees, venues and operating rules are not final and remain subject to approval.
